Interview Tips

Stay Focused: Maintain eye contact with the camera and speak clearly. This helps create a more professional and engaging interview experience.

Take Your Time: Don't rush your answers. It's okay to take a moment to gather your thoughts before responding to each question.

Structure Your Answers: Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) when applicable to provide clear, well-organized responses.

Remember: This is a practice session! Stay calm, be yourself, and use this opportunity to improve your interviewing skills.
